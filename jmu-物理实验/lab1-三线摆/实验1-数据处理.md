### 3. 计算空悬盘的转动惯量 $I_0$ 及其不确定度

单位换算（SI 单位制）：

$$
\begin{aligned}
m &= 355\ \text{g} = 0.355\ \text{kg} \\
\bar{a} &= 4.709333\ \text{cm} = 0.047093\ \text{m} \\
\bar{b} &= 11.580667\ \text{cm} = 0.115807\ \text{m} \\
\bar{H}_0 &= 49.58125\ \text{cm} = 0.495812\ \text{m} \\
\bar{t}_0 &= 83.595\ \text{s} \\
g &= 9.80\ \text{m/s}^2
\end{aligned}
$$

周期：

$$
T_0 = \frac{\bar{t}_0}{50} = \frac{83.595}{50} = 1.671900\ \text{s}
$$

$$
T_0^2 = 2.79525\ \text{s}^2
$$

转动惯量 $I_0$：

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_0 &= \frac{mg\bar{a}\bar{b}}{12\pi^2 \bar{H}_0} \cdot T_0^2 \\[4pt]
&= \frac{0.355 \times 9.80 \times 0.047093 \times 0.115807}{12 \times \pi^2 \times 0.495812} \times 2.79525 \\[4pt]
&= \frac{0.0189735}{58.7217} \times 2.79525 \\[4pt]
&= 3.2311 \times 10^{-4} \times 2.79525 \\[4pt]
&= \boxed{9.03 \times 10^{-4}\ \text{kg}\cdot\text{m}^2}
\end{aligned}
$$

不确定度 $u_{I_0}$：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{u_a}{\bar{a}} &= \frac{0.01971}{4.70933} = 4.19 \times 10^{-3} \\[4pt]
\frac{u_b}{\bar{b}} &= \frac{0.02370}{11.5807} = 2.05 \times 10^{-3} \\[4pt]
\frac{u_{H_0}}{\bar{H}_0} &= \frac{0.02975}{49.5813} = 6.00 \times 10^{-4} \\[4pt]
\frac{2u_{t_0}}{\bar{t}_0} &= \frac{2 \times 0.05047}{83.595} = 1.21 \times 10^{-3}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
u_{I_0} &= I_0 \sqrt{ \left(\frac{u_a}{\bar{a}}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{u_b}{\bar{b}}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{u_{H_0}}{\bar{H}_0}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{2u_{t_0}}{\bar{t}_0}\right)^2 } \\[4pt]
&= 9.03 \times 10^{-4} \times \sqrt{(4.19 \times 10^{-3})^2 + (2.05 \times 10^{-3})^2 + (6.00 \times 10^{-4})^2 + (1.21 \times 10^{-3})^2} \\[4pt]
&= 9.03 \times 10^{-4} \times \sqrt{1.75 \times 10^{-5} + 4.19 \times 10^{-6} + 3.60 \times 10^{-7} + 1.46 \times 10^{-6}} \\[4pt]
&= 9.03 \times 10^{-4} \times 4.85 \times 10^{-3} \\[4pt]
&= \boxed{4.4 \times 10^{-6}\ \text{kg}\cdot\text{m}^2}
\end{aligned}
$$

### 4. 计算载物悬盘的转动惯量 $I_1$ 及其不确定度

单位换算：

$$
\begin{aligned}
m &= 0.355\ \text{kg} \\
M &= 0.49584\ \text{kg} \\
M + m &= 0.85084\ \text{kg} \\
\bar{H}_1 &= 49.8375\ \text{cm} = 0.498375\ \text{m} \\
\bar{t}_1 &= 69.13125\ \text{s}
\end{aligned}
$$

周期：

$$
T_1 = \frac{\bar{t}_1}{50} = \frac{69.13125}{50} = 1.38263\ \text{s}
$$

$$
T_1^2 = 1.91165\ \text{s}^2
$$

转动惯量 $I_1$：

$$
\begin{aligned}
I_1 &= \frac{(M+m)g\bar{a}\bar{b}}{12\pi^2 \bar{H}_1} \cdot T_1^2 \\[4pt]
&= \frac{0.85084 \times 9.80 \times 0.047093 \times 0.115807}{12 \times \pi^2 \times 0.498375} \times 1.91165 \\[4pt]
&= \frac{0.0454744}{59.0252} \times 1.91165 \\[4pt]
&= 7.7042 \times 10^{-4} \times 1.91165 \\[4pt]
&= \boxed{1.47 \times 10^{-3}\ \text{kg}\cdot\text{m}^2}
\end{aligned}
$$

不确定度 $u_{I_1}$：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{u_a}{\bar{a}} &= 4.19 \times 10^{-3} \\[4pt]
\frac{u_b}{\bar{b}} &= 2.05 \times 10^{-3} \\[4pt]
\frac{u_{H_1}}{\bar{H}_1} &= \frac{0.03174}{49.8375} = 6.37 \times 10^{-4} \\[4pt]
\frac{2u_{t_1}}{\bar{t}_1} &= \frac{2 \times 0.04671}{69.1313} = 1.35 \times 10^{-3} \\[4pt]
\frac{u_M}{M+m} &= \frac{0.02887}{850.84} = 3.39 \times 10^{-5}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
u_{I_1} &= I_1 \sqrt{ \left(\frac{u_a}{\bar{a}}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{u_b}{\bar{b}}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{u_{H_1}}{\bar{H}_1}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{2u_{t_1}}{\bar{t}_1}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{u_M}{M+m}\right)^2 } \\[4pt]
&= 1.47 \times 10^{-3} \times \sqrt{1.75 \times 10^{-5} + 4.19 \times 10^{-6} + 4.06 \times 10^{-7} + 1.83 \times 10^{-6} + 1.15 \times 10^{-9}} \\[4pt]
&= 1.47 \times 10^{-3} \times 4.89 \times 10^{-3} \\[4pt]
&= \boxed{7.2 \times 10^{-6}\ \text{kg}\cdot\text{m}^2}
\end{aligned}
$$

---

### 5. 计算不规则测件的转动惯量 $I$ 及其不确定度

$$
I = I_1 - I_0 = 1.4728 \times 10^{-3} - 9.03 \times 10^{-4} = \boxed{5.70 \times 10^{-4}\ \text{kg}\cdot\text{m}^2}
$$

$$
u_I = \sqrt{u_{I_0}^2 + u_{I_1}^2} = \sqrt{(4.4 \times 10^{-6})^2 + (7.2 \times 10^{-6})^2} = \boxed{8.4 \times 10^{-6}\ \text{kg}\cdot\text{m}^2}
$$

---

### 6. 实验结果的表达

三线摆法测定不规则物体的转动惯量的测量结果为：

$$
\boxed{I = \bar{I} \pm u_I = (5.70 \times 10^{-4} \pm 9 \times 10^{-6})\ \text{kg}\cdot\text{m}^2,\qquad (P = 68.3\%)}
$$

> 注：不确定度保留 1 个有效数字，遵循"逢数进位"原则（$8.4 \times 10^{-6} \to 9 \times 10^{-6}$）；平均值结果的尾数与不确定度的尾数对齐（$10^{-6}$ 位），遵循"四舍六入五凑偶"原则。

## 一、实验数据与处理

### （一）测球体的转动惯量

#### 1. 各直接测量量平均值及其不确定度的计算

仪器误差：
- 电子天平：Δ = 0.05 g
- 游标卡尺：Δ = 0.02 mm
- 秒表：Δ = 0.05 s

---

##### 对 M

M 为单次测量，直接取测量值。

\[
\bar{M} = 358.43\ \mathrm{g}
\]

\[
u_M = \frac{\Delta}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{0.05}{\sqrt{3}} = 0.029\ \mathrm{g}
\]

\[
E_M = \frac{u_M}{\bar{M}} = \frac{0.029}{358.43} = 0.0081\%
\]

---

##### 对 D

D 为 8 次测量，经肖维涅准则检验未发现异常值，n = 8。

\[
\bar{D} = 10.03075\ \mathrm{cm},\quad S_D = 0.00183\ \mathrm{cm}
\]

\[
u_A = \frac{S_D}{\sqrt{n}} = \frac{0.00183}{\sqrt{8}} = 0.00065\ \mathrm{cm}
\]

\[
u_B = \frac{\Delta}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{0.002}{\sqrt{3}} = 0.00115\ \mathrm{cm}
\]

\[
u_D = \sqrt{u_A^2 + u_B^2} = \sqrt{0.00065^2 + 0.00115^2} = 0.0013\ \mathrm{cm}
\]

\[
E_D = \frac{u_D}{\bar{D}} = \frac{0.0013}{10.03075} = 0.013\%
\]

---

##### 对 t₁

t₁ 为 8 次测量，经肖维涅准则检验未发现异常值，n = 8。

\[
\bar{t}_1 = 13.00375\ \mathrm{s},\quad S_{t_1} = 0.05181\ \mathrm{s}
\]

\[
u_A = \frac{S_{t_1}}{\sqrt{n}} = \frac{0.05181}{\sqrt{8}} = 0.0183\ \mathrm{s}
\]

\[
u_B = \frac{\Delta}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{0.05}{\sqrt{3}} = 0.0289\ \mathrm{s}
\]

\[
u_{t_1} = \sqrt{u_A^2 + u_B^2} = \sqrt{0.0183^2 + 0.0289^2} = 0.034\ \mathrm{s}
\]

\[
E_{t_1} = \frac{u_{t_1}}{\bar{t}_1} = \frac{0.034}{13.00375} = 0.26\%
\]

---

##### 对 t₂

t₂ 为 8 次测量，经肖维涅准则检验剔除坏值 18.25 s，有效数据 n = 7。

\[
\bar{t}_2 = 18.37714\ \mathrm{s},\quad S_{t_2} = 0.03498\ \mathrm{s}
\]

\[
u_A = \frac{S_{t_2}}{\sqrt{n}} = \frac{0.03498}{\sqrt{7}} = 0.0132\ \mathrm{s}
\]

\[
u_B = \frac{\Delta}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{0.05}{\sqrt{3}} = 0.0289\ \mathrm{s}
\]

\[
u_{t_2} = \sqrt{u_A^2 + u_B^2} = \sqrt{0.0132^2 + 0.0289^2} = 0.032\ \mathrm{s}
\]

\[
E_{t_2} = \frac{u_{t_2}}{\bar{t}_2} = \frac{0.032}{18.37714} = 0.17\%
\]

---

##### 对 t

t 为 8 次测量，经肖维涅准则检验未发现异常值，n = 8。

\[
\bar{t} = 25.44375\ \mathrm{s},\quad S_t = 0.08943\ \mathrm{s}
\]

\[
u_A = \frac{S_t}{\sqrt{n}} = \frac{0.08943}{\sqrt{8}} = 0.0316\ \mathrm{s}
\]

\[
u_B = \frac{\Delta}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{0.05}{\sqrt{3}} = 0.0289\ \mathrm{s}
\]

\[
u_t = \sqrt{u_A^2 + u_B^2} = \sqrt{0.0316^2 + 0.0289^2} = 0.043\ \mathrm{s}
\]

\[
E_t = \frac{u_t}{\bar{t}} = \frac{0.043}{25.44375} = 0.17\%
\]

#### 2. 物体转动惯量的计算

##### （1）圆柱体转动惯量

\[
\begin{aligned}
I_0 &= \frac{1}{8} M \bar{D}^2 \\[4pt]
    &= \frac{1}{8} \times 0.35843 \times (0.1003075)^2 \\[4pt]
    &= 4.508 \times 10^{-4}\ \mathrm{kg \cdot m^2}
\end{aligned}
\]

\[
\begin{aligned}
E_{I_0} &= \sqrt{E_M^2 + (2E_D)^2} \times 100\% \\[4pt]
        &= \sqrt{(0.0081\%)^2 + (2 \times 0.013\%)^2} \times 100\% \\[4pt]
        &= \sqrt{6.56 \times 10^{-7} + 6.76 \times 10^{-6}} \times 100\% \\[4pt]
        &= 0.028\%
\end{aligned}
\]

##### （2）球体的转动惯量

由公式 \( K = \dfrac{4\pi^2 I_0}{T_2^2 - T_1^2} \)，代入 \( I = \dfrac{K}{4\pi^2} T^2 \) 可推得：

\[
\bar{I} = \frac{\bar{T}^2}{\bar{T}_2^2 - \bar{T}_1^2} I_0
       = \frac{\bar{t}^2}{\bar{t}_2^2 - \bar{t}_1^2} I_0
\]

\[
\begin{aligned}
\bar{t}^2          &= 25.44375^2 = 647.384 \\[4pt]
\bar{t}_2^2        &= 18.37714^2 = 337.719 \\[4pt]
\bar{t}_1^2        &= 13.00375^2 = 169.098 \\[4pt]
\bar{t}_2^2 - \bar{t}_1^2 &= 337.719 - 169.098 = 168.621
\end{aligned}
\]

\[
\begin{aligned}
\bar{I} &= \frac{647.384}{168.621} \times 4.508 \times 10^{-4} \\[4pt]
        &= 3.839 \times 4.508 \times 10^{-4} \\[4pt]
        &= 1.731 \times 10^{-3}\ \mathrm{kg \cdot m^2}
\end{aligned}
\]

##### （3）球体转动惯量不确定度

令 \( Y = t_2^2 - t_1^2 \)，则：

\[
\bar{Y} = \bar{t}_2^2 - \bar{t}_1^2 = 168.621
\]

\[
\begin{aligned}
u_Y &= \sqrt{ (2\bar{t}_2 u_{t_2})^2 + (2\bar{t}_1 u_{t_1})^2 } \\[4pt]
    &= \sqrt{ (2 \times 18.377 \times 0.032)^2 + (2 \times 13.004 \times 0.034)^2 } \\[4pt]
    &= \sqrt{ 1.383 + 0.782 } \\[4pt]
    &= 1.471
\end{aligned}
\]

\[
\begin{aligned}
E_I &= \sqrt{ \left( \frac{2u_t}{\bar{t}} \right)^2
           + \left( \frac{u_Y}{\bar{Y}} \right)^2
           + E_{I_0}^2 } \times 100\% \\[6pt]
    &= \sqrt{ \left( \frac{2 \times 0.043}{25.444} \right)^2
           + \left( \frac{1.471}{168.621} \right)^2
           + (0.028\%)^2 } \times 100\% \\[6pt]
    &= \sqrt{ 1.14 \times 10^{-5} + 7.62 \times 10^{-5} + 7.42 \times 10^{-8} } \times 100\% \\[6pt]
    &= 0.94\%
\end{aligned}
\]

\[
u_I = E_I \, \bar{I} = 0.0094 \times 1.731 \times 10^{-3} = 1.6 \times 10^{-5}\ \mathrm{kg \cdot m^2}
\]

##### 实验结果表达

扭摆法测定球体转动惯量的测量结果为：

\[
\boxed{I = (1.73 \pm 0.02) \times 10^{-3}\ \mathrm{kg \cdot m^2} \qquad (P = 68.3\%)}
\]

> 注：
> - 不确定度保留 1 位有效数字，按"逢数进位"原则：\( 1.6 \times 10^{-5} \to 2 \times 10^{-5} \)
> - 平均值尾数与不确定度尾数对齐，按"四舍六入五凑偶"原则：\( 1.731 \times 10^{-3} \to 1.73 \times 10^{-3} \)

### （二）验证平行轴定理

先计算转轴及挂上对称滑块的细杆的转动惯量（以下以空杆为例给出其中一组计算过程）：

由扭摆周期公式 \( T = 2\pi \sqrt{\dfrac{I}{K}} \) 得：

\[
I = \frac{K}{4\pi^2} T^2
\]

由（一）中结果，扭摆扭转常数：

\[
K = \frac{4\pi^2 I_0}{T_2^2 - T_1^2}
   = \frac{4\pi^2 \times 4.508 \times 10^{-4}}{\left(\dfrac{18.377}{20}\right)^2 - \left(\dfrac{13.004}{20}\right)^2}
   = 0.0422\ \mathrm{N \cdot m}
\]

空杆转动惯量（10 个周期，\( T = \dfrac{19.78}{10} = 1.978\ \mathrm{s} \)）：

\[
I_{\text{空杆}} = \frac{K}{4\pi^2} T^2
               = \frac{0.0422}{39.478} \times 1.978^2
               = 4.18 \times 10^{-3}\ \mathrm{kg \cdot m^2}
\]

同理可计算挂滑块于各位置时系统的总转动惯量，进而得到不同 \( x \) 处滑块附加转动惯量 \( I_{\text{滑块}} = I_{\text{总}} - I_{\text{空杆}} \)。

再使用**作图法**做出 \( I \)–\( X^2 \) 的关系曲线：

| 位置 \( x \) (cm) | 5 | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| \( x^2 \) (cm²) | 25 | 100 | 225 | 400 | 625 |
| 时间 \( t \) (s) | 22.47 | 28.88 | 37.72 | 46.75 | 56.82 |
| 周期 \( T \) (s) | 2.247 | 2.888 | 3.772 | 4.675 | 5.682 |
| \( I_{\text{总}} \) (kg·m²) | | | | | |

> 注：请使用坐标纸规范作图，以 \( X^2 \) 为横轴、\( I_{\text{滑块}} \) 为纵轴绘制散点图并拟合直线，验证平行轴定理。
